## 🔑 Key Insights

1. [Auth Pattern] API_Key dominates (77/100 apps) with OAuth2 close behind (61/100), but 23 apps use Basic_Auth requiring credential exposure mitigation. 15 apps use hybrid auth (multi-method) needing flexible toolkit auth handling.

2. [Gating Pattern] Self-serve is easiest in Messaging, Ecommerce, DevTools, Productivity, AI/Media (100% accessible), hardest in Data/SEO (60% accessible). Data/SEO apps gating APIs behind expensive enterprise subscriptions is the biggest barrier.

3. [Blocker Pattern] Top blocker: "Enterprise/Partner-gated API access" (8 apps) — requires Composio partnership outreach. Marketo, Kayako, and Gladly remain fully Blocked behind sales contracts.

4. [Easy Wins] 51 apps are "Ready" with broad APIs + self-serve OAuth2 — prioritize these for Composio toolkit builds. DevTools (10/10) and Ecommerce (10/10) categories are 100% ready for immediate integration.

5. [MCP Signal] Only 24 apps explicitly mention agent/MCP/tool-calling support — a massive opportunity for Composio to lead agent-readiness for the remaining 76 apps. DevTools (6 apps) and AI/Media (5 apps) lead MCP adoption.
